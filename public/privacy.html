Privacy Policy for App Blueprint

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

Zero-Data Collection Guarantee App Blueprint was engineered with a strict, privacy-by-design local architecture. We do not collect, track, log, transmit, or monetize your personal information. All your app concepts, checklist progress, notes, and generated configuration files remain 100% on your local device. We run zero analytics servers, zero telemetry SDKs, and zero tracking cookies.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

App Blueprint operates strictly as a local, client-side utility. Specifically:

  • No Accounts or Logins: You are not required to provide your name, email address, password, phone number, or any personal credentials to use App Blueprint.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or Amplitude). We do not record screens, user taps, or session data.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not read or access your Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or Google Advertising ID (GAID).
  • No Remote Databases: We do not operate remote servers or cloud databases that ingest your proprietary app blueprints, notes, or checklist entries.

2. Local Storage & On-Device Data

All application state is stored strictly in your device’s isolated sandboxed storage (using standard WebKit/browser localStorage APIs). This includes:

  • Your projects list, titles, descriptions, and custom color tags;
  • Your checklist completion states across the 7 architecture phases;
  • Custom checklist tasks you create;
  • Local user preference toggles and legal agreement acknowledgements.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it yourself using system share dialogs.

3. Configuration Generators & File Exports

App Blueprint includes client-side generators for iOS Privacy Manifests (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy), entitlements, and configuration templates. All file compilation and exports take place entirely in your local browser runtime via JavaScript Blobs. No files or configuration data are uploaded to or processed by any external server.

4. Third-Party Links & Documentation

The App includes curated reference links to external developer documentation, such as Apple Developer documentation, Human Interface Guidelines (HIG), and official open-source specifications. Tapping an external link opens the destination in your default web browser, subject to the privacy policy of that third-party website.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

App Blueprint does not knowingly collect, request, or retain personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the App collects zero personal data, it complies fully with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and European General Data Protection Regulation requirements.

6. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively in your device’s local sandbox:

  • You can delete individual projects at any time using the in-app Project Management menu.
  • You can wipe all data by clearing website storage / app cache in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the App Blueprint application from your device permanently and irrevocably deletes all locally stored data.

7. Compliance with App Store Review Guidelines

This policy complies with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 (Data Collection and Storage) and Guideline 5.1.2 (Data Use and Sharing). App Blueprint collects zero user data and requires zero tracking permissions.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

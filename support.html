Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my project data stored?
All your projects, checklist states, and notes are stored strictly on your local device. App Blueprint does not sync or store your data on external cloud servers, guaranteeing complete privacy for your unpublished app concepts.
How do I create and manage multiple apps?
Tap the Folder icon in the navigation bar to open the Projects manager. From there, you can create new projects, assign color tags, switch active projects, or delete completed ones.
How do I export configuration files like Privacy manifests?
Navigate to the Resources tab, choose Config Generators, fill in your app details, and tap 'Copy XML/JSON' or 'Download' to get instant, store-ready configuration files.
How do I report a bug or request a guideline update?
Email us at support@appblueprint.app with the guideline name or description, and our engineering team will review it for the next release.

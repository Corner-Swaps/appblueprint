Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is App Blueprint?
App Blueprint is an architecture scaffolding, checklist verification, and store readiness platform built for iOS and cross-platform mobile developers. It organizes app development into 7 strategic phases—from initial scope and Apple Human Interface Guidelines (HIG) compliance through architecture, security, performance, and App Store submission.
2. Where are my projects and checklists stored? Is my app idea private?
Your data is 100% private and stored exclusively on your local device using isolated local sandbox storage (localStorage). App Blueprint does not operate remote databases or user-harvesting servers. Your proprietary app concepts and architecture notes never leave your device.
3. How is the Launch Readiness Score calculated?
Your Launch Readiness Score reflects the percentage of essential architecture and store submission requirements completed across all 7 development phases. It accounts for both standard system checklist items and any custom tasks you add to your project.
4. How do I generate and export Privacy Manifests (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy)?
Navigate to the Resources section and select Config Generators. Select your required API usage reasons and tracking declarations, then tap Copy XML or Download. The file is generated instantaneously on your device and is ready to be dropped into your Xcode project.
5. Can I manage multiple mobile apps simultaneously?
Yes. Tap the Folder icon in the navigation bar to open the Project Manager. You can create separate blueprints for different apps, customize color badges, switch between active projects with a tap, or remove archived apps.
6. Does App Blueprint guarantee that Apple or Google will approve my app?
No. App Blueprint provides educational guidelines, industry best practices, and organizational checklists to help you identify common traps. However, final app review decisions are determined solely by Apple Inc. and Google LLC at their own discretion. You are solely responsible for ensuring that your app complies with platform review policies.
7. How do I report an issue or suggest new checklist items?
Please email us directly at goloubov@gmail.com with details of the issue or feature recommendation. We regularly update the checklist to reflect Apple's latest WWDC and App Store Review guidelines.

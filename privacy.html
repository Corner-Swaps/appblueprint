Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy for App Blueprint

Effective Date: September 22, 2026

Zero-Data Collection Guarantee: App Blueprint was built with a strict privacy-first architecture. All your checklist progress, custom projects, generated configurations, and notes remain 100% on your local device. We do not operate remote user tracking servers, do not sell your personal data, and do not include third-party ad networks.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

App Blueprint does not collect, transmit, store, or share personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or create a password to use App Blueprint.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not track your in-app actions, user behavior, or unique advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID).
  • No Cloud Storage of App Data: All checklists, notes, project records, and generator configurations are stored exclusively on your device using local sandboxed storage.

2. Local On-Device Data Storage

App Blueprint stores your custom settings, created projects, and checklist verification progress locally on your device using local storage and sandboxed application cache. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself using standard operating system share sheets.

3. Third-Party Services & External Links

The application contains helpful curated external hyperlinks to official developer documentation (such as Apple Developer Guidelines, Human Interface Guidelines, and Google Play Console policies). Tapping an external link opens that web page in your default browser, subject to the privacy policy and terms of that external destination.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

App Blueprint does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal data from anyone under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted by the app, App Blueprint complies fully with COPPA and global child privacy regulations.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your local device:

  • You can reset or delete any individual project directly inside the app using the Project Management menu.
  • Uninstalling the App Blueprint application immediately and permanently removes all locally stored data, checklists, and project records from your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect future features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
